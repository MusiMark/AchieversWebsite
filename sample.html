About Achievers Uganda

Discover our journey, mission, and the passionate team behind Uganda's youth transformation.

Our beginning

A movement that started with a simple observation.

In 2018, our founder saw a recurring gap among talented Ugandan youth: they had dreams but lacked the mindset, emotional tools, and mentorship to turn those dreams into reality. Low confidence, poor decision-making, and difficulty coping with failure often led to unemployment and unrealized potential.

Achievers Uganda began with small community workshops in Kampala. We now equip young people aged 10–25 with hands-on learning in growth mindset, emotional intelligence, and leadership.

Our mission

To equip young people in Uganda with the life skills, growth mindset, and emotional intelligence they need to become confident, purpose-driven leaders who thrive in school, work, and life.

Our vision

A generation of empowered young achievers who break cycles of poverty and limitation, transforming their communities and the nation through mindset-driven success.

The challenge

Young people need more than a classroom can provide.

Uganda has one of the youngest populations in the world, yet many youth leave school without critical soft skills such as self-belief, emotional regulation, and goal-setting. We make these practical skills accessible, relatable, and lasting.

  • Mindset training for real life
  • Emotional resilience and wellbeing
  • Leadership that serves communities

We meet young people where they are, then help them see how far they can go.
