📌 Transition Workshop 2026

New Level, Bigger Dreams: Bridge the Gap to High School Success!

Primary Seven (P.7) to Senior One (S.1) — A 2-Day Transformative Experience

About the Event

Give your child the ultimate head start for high school. Moving to secondary school comes with new peer pressures, complex social dynamics, and tough academic demands. This high-impact 2-day workshop equips P.7 vacists with unwavering self-confidence, emotional intelligence, and bully-proofing skills to transition smoothly, make smart friendships, and excel in academics.

What your child will gain:

  • Bully-proofing skills to handle peer pressure confidently
  • Emotional intelligence for better decision-making and relationships
  • Strategies to form smart, uplifting friendships
  • A growth mindset and goal-setting habits for academic excellence
  • Unwavering self-confidence to step into Senior One
  • Social navigation skills for the complex secondary school environment

Spaces are limited to ensure personalized life coaching. Secure your child's spot today to get lessons of what is not taught in class!
