🎓 TRANSITION WORKSHOP 2026

New Level, Bigger Dreams: Bridge the Gap to High School Success

Primary Seven (P.7) to Senior One (S.1)

About the Event

Give your child the ultimate head start for high school. Moving to secondary school comes with new peer pressures, complex social dynamics, and tough academic demands. This high-impact 2-day workshop equips P.7 vacists with unwavering self-confidence, emotional intelligence, and bully-proofing skills.

What your child will gain:

  • Bully-proofing skills to handle peer pressure
  • Emotional intelligence for better decisions
  • Strategies to form smart friendships
  • Growth mindset & goal-setting habits
  • Unwavering self-confidence for S.1
  • Social navigation skills for secondary school
