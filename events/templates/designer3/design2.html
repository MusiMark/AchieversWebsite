New Level, Bigger Dreams: Bridge the Gap to High School Success

Transition Workshop · Primary Seven to Senior One

About the event

Two days to walk in ready, not just show up

Moving to secondary school comes with new peer pressures, complex social dynamics, and tough academic demands.

This high-impact 2-day workshop equips P.7 leavers with unwavering self-confidence, emotional intelligence, and bully-proofing skills to transition smoothly, make smart friendships, and excel academically. Spaces are limited to ensure personalised life coaching — secure your child's spot to get the lessons that aren't taught in class.

  • Bully-proofing skills to handle peer pressure confidently
  • Emotional intelligence for better decisions and relationships
  • Strategies to form smart, uplifting friendships
  • A growth mindset and goal-setting habits for academic excellence
  • Unwavering self-confidence to step into Senior One
  • Social navigation skills for the secondary school environment
