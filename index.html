Our Story

Who Are We?

Achievers Mindset Program (AMP) is a youth empowerment initiative based in Uganda. We help young people develop essential life skills, emotional intelligence, and a growth mindset through workshops, mentorship, and community outreach.

Our goal: to equip every young person with the confidence and purpose to succeed — no matter their background.
